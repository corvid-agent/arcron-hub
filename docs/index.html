WHAT IS ARCRON cron, but the chain is the clock and the crowd is the crontab

Arcron is a permissionless keeper network on Algorand. Anyone registers a recurring upkeep: a target application call, an interval in rounds, and a fee escrow. When the upkeep falls due, any keeper on the network may execute it and claim the fee from escrow. Keepers race; the winner is paid, losers pay nothing beyond their own (refunded-safe, fee-covered) transaction cost. No allowlists, no operator keys, no gatekeepers.
